Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?
Yes. Click More info, then Run anyway. This shows for internal software that is not sold in public stores.
Do I need administrator rights?
No. Rasumi Apps installs for your Windows user only. If your PC is locked down, contact IT.
I already have an older version.
Install over it. Your settings stay, and later updates install by themselves.
Which e-mail do I sign up with?
The one this invitation was sent to: . Other e-mails cannot sign up.
Can I open this page on another computer?
You can read it anywhere, but after your first download it works in that browser on that PC only. If you changed PC, ask IT to reset your invitation.
