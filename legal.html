Compliance & Security

Information Security Statement (ISMS Policy)

Rasumi Group is committed to maintaining a robust Information Security Management System (ISMS) aligned with internationally recognized standards, including ISO/IEC 27001 principles.

Our information security objectives are to ensure:

  • Confidentiality – Information is accessible only to authorized individuals
  • Integrity – Information is accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorized modification
  • Availability – Systems and data are accessible when required by authorized users

Security Governance & Controls

We implement a risk-based approach to information security, supported by:

  • Formalized security policies, procedures, and internal controls
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) and least-privilege principles
  • Secure system architecture and controlled environments
  • Continuous monitoring, logging, and audit trails
  • Periodic risk assessments and internal reviews

Responsible Disclosure / Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (VDP)

Rasumi Group welcomes responsible security research and is committed to maintaining the security and integrity of its systems.

Authorized Testing Guidelines: Security researchers must act in good faith, avoid privacy violations, and immediately cease testing upon discovery of sensitive data exposure.

Prohibited Activities: Social engineering, physical security testing, and Denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) are strictly prohibited.

Business Continuity & Incident Response

Aligned with ISO 22301 principles, our approach includes identification of critical functions, impact analysis (BIA), and defined recovery strategies. We maintain an incident response framework designed to detect, contain, and investigate security or operational incidents.

Third-Party Vendor Risk Management

We implement controls to ensure that all vendors are assessed prior to engagement and meet minimum security, compliance, and regulatory standards.
